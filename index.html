Group Mode
How playdates work
You stay in control at every step
1
Become friends
You and the other parent connect your families. Friendship alone shares nothing.
2
Both parents allow voice
Group voice only turns on when both of you flip the consent toggle for that family.
3
Tap the cubes together
NFC confirms the cubes are physically near — it’s a proximity check, not consent.
4
Short, visible listening
The cube shows it’s listening. Audio is processed briefly, then deleted — nothing is stored.
5
Stop anytime
Either parent can end it; revoking consent blocks future sessions instantly.
No recordings, no profiles of other children, no training on this data. Ever.
